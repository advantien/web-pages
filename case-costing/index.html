Set up the estimate

Describe the case. Decide what counts as a match.

Link a scheduled case to pre-fill what’s already known, or enter the details yourself. Beyond the surgeon and CPT codes, everything is optional.

Payer or payer category, one click for Commercial, Medicare, Medicaid, Self-pay and more.
“Must” switches make any input a hard requirement, like only this surgeon or every code.
Minimum match score, with a live count of how many cases each setting keeps, and an optional date range.
Implant expectations: name the manufacturer or known implants, or enter the rep’s quote as the implant total.
01 · Instant estimates

A full profit and loss for the case, before it’s on the schedule.

Every estimate is built from the facility’s real past cases, not benchmarks, and each line shows the realistic range around it.

Every line of the P&L: expected revenue, implant cost, other direct costs, gross profit, indirect costs and net profit.
Revenue follows the payer. Revenue can come from that payer’s cases across all surgeons, so it reflects the contract.
Costs follow the surgeon, drawn from their own cases, with the likely implant list and unit costs.

Three ways to read the estimate

Conservative

Pairs lower revenue with higher costs, for a cautious number to plan around.

Median

The middle of your matched cases, for revenue and for every cost line.

Optimistic

Pairs higher revenue with lower costs, to show the upside when a case goes your way.

02 · Best-match scoring

Every past case scored for how closely it matches.

COREintel weighs the things that change a case’s cost, so the estimate rests on cases that really are like the one you’re planning.

Scored on what matters: primary CPT and other codes, surgeon, payer, procedure description, implants used and how recent the case is.
Nothing hidden. Every case shows its score and how well it matched, and opens to its implant lines.
Works for newer surgeons too. With fewer than five matching cases, it draws on other surgeons’ cases for the same work.
03 · Grouped by implants

See why two similar-looking cases cost differently.

COREintel groups like surgeries by the implants they used, so a wide cost range turns into a few clear patterns you can plan around.

Lettered clusters, such as anchors only, anchors plus a patch, or anchors plus a graft, each with its typical cost and range.
Which items, or how many? See how much of the spread comes from the implants chosen, and how much from the count.
Cost per extra anchor or screw, shown when the unit count explains the difference.
04 · Share in one click

Share a clean picture with the surgeon.

“Copy picture” turns the estimate into an image sized for email, ready to paste into a message to a surgeon, scheduler or board member.

Everything that matters: the best estimate, typical and full ranges, the clusters and the likely implant list.
No patient details. Dates of service, record numbers and payer names are never included.
